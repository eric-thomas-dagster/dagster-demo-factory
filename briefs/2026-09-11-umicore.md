# Umicore — demo brief

## Demo thesis

Umicore runs a deliberate data-mesh architecture across four business units
with no data-aware orchestrator underneath it — data refreshes on a fixed
clock instead of on the actual completion of upstream work, so a late job
leaves everything downstream stale until the next scheduled window, and at
least one team's workflow is broken on any given day with no central place to
see it. The open question isn't whether Dagster is technically capable, it's
whether a data mesh built from independently-owned business-unit data
products can get a *single* lineage graph and event-based recovery without
displacing the Stonebranch/Ansible infrastructure-scheduling investment they
made two months ago. This demo has to prove coexistence is real, not a
compromise: Stonebranch stays master for infra scheduling, Dagster owns data
freshness, quality, and cross-business-unit lineage, and a Stonebranch-
triggered job shows up in the Dagster graph as an observed asset rather than a
black box. If that lands, the second half of the pitch — that this reliability
is what makes their EU Battery Regulation carbon-footprint reporting
defensible — closes the business case someone still has to take to Umicore's
new Chief Digital & Transformation Officer.
