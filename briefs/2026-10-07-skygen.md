- **Blocking eligibility check:** if a client's eligibility feed is late or empty in production, downstream claims deliverables will not compute, instead of shipping wrong numbers to a payer.
- **Replication reconciliation:** a replication job reporting success is not the same as correct data; this asserts it.
- **Freshness policy on payer deliverables:** answers "how would we know a client's extract is stale".
- **Eager automation:** curated tables recompute when upstream replication lands and the gate passes; no cron chain to babysit.
- **Dagster+ alerting:** native routing to Slack/Teams/PagerDuty per team; no custom alerting built.
- **Observation sensor:** replication runs triggered outside Dagster still show up in the graph.
