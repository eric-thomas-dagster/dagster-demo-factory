## Demo thesis

Skygen (dental/vision SaaS + TPA) is moving ~170 per-client SQL Server
databases, run today on SSIS and PowerShell, onto Snowflake, Fivetran and dbt.
A six-person team has no single view, and "customers spot SLA misses first."
This demo has to prove that one code definition fanned out across tenants gives
them per-tenant lineage from Fivetran to dbt to Sigma, quality gates, freshness,
and selective reruns, so a change reaches all 170 tenants without 170 configs
and a stale tenant is found by them, not the customer. It also has to answer
Jean's "why not Terraform?" directly: Terraform stands infrastructure up; it
doesn't tell you which tenant is stale or rerun just the broken step.
